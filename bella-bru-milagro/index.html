Questions

Milagro Event Center FAQ

How many guests can Milagro Event Center hold?

The full event space seats 200 guests with a dance floor, 240 seated without a dance floor, or 300 for a cocktail-style event. The partial space seats 60, or 75 for cocktails.

How much does it cost to rent Milagro Event Center?

Full site rental is $3,500 on Saturdays, $3,000 on Fridays and Sundays, and $2,500 Monday through Thursday. Partial rental for 25 to 60 guests is $250 per hour with a 5 hour minimum, Monday through Thursday or before 3:00 pm on Fridays and Sundays. Additional rooms are $50 per hour. Tax, a 20% service charge and a 4% admin fee apply.

Is there a food and beverage minimum?

Yes. The food and beverage minimum is $25 per person or $625, whichever is greater. All food and beverage is provided by Bella Bru.

Can we bring our own caterer?

All food and beverage at Milagro is provided by Bella Bru, with appetizer, reception, dessert and bar packages to choose from.

What is included with a full site rental?

Event staff for up to 6 hours, serviceware and glassware, linens, custom lighting, a private patio with fireplace and water feature, clear chiavari chairs and silver chiavari barstools, round, king and cocktail tables with a custom floorplan, a sound system with handheld microphones, an LED TV wall with 2 projector screens, and free WiFi.

Is parking available?

Yes. Milagro offers free parking for 100+ vehicles. Valet parking may be required for Friday and Saturday events with 150 or more guests.

Can we serve alcohol at our event?

Yes. Bar packages are available, from non-alcoholic to premium liquor, plus cash bar and drink ticket options. Security is required for events with alcohol at $35 per hour.

Do we need event insurance?

Yes. Event insurance is required for all events.

Where is Milagro Event Center?

Milagro Event Center by Bella Bru is at 6241 Fair Oaks Blvd, Suite D, Carmichael, CA 95608, in the Sacramento area.

Can we see the space before booking?

Yes. Call (916) 704-9749 to set up a visit.
