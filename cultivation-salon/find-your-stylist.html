Good to know

Why does each stylist have their own booking page?

Some of our stylists run their own books. Every button on this page takes you straight to the right calendar for that stylist, so you never have to hunt for it.

What does it cost?

Haircuts start at $80, highlights and balayage start at $180, and root touch-ups start at $150. Prices vary by stylist and hair length.

Where do I park?

Free 2-hour street parking is within a block of 2512 J Street, and paid parking is right out front.
