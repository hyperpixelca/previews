Our goals

  1. Grow East Lawn's share of families
    Reach more families searching for a funeral home or cemetery who don't already know East Lawn by name.
  2. 150 to 250 calls a month from the ads
    A call counts when it lasts 30 seconds or longer.
  3. $19,500 target ad spend each month
    Money goes to the ads that bring calls at the lowest cost.

Reaching new families

  • Did not search East Lawn by name
  • Searched East Lawn by name

In October, about 163 calls came from families who were not searching for East Lawn by name. April to September averaged 94 a month.

On the main ad campaign, ads shown to these families went from 1,144 in September to 4,591 in October, about 4 times as many.

What we changed and why

  1. Aimed the ads at phone calls
    Google was chasing website page views; now it looks for families who call.
  2. Cut wasted spend
    Blocked junk apps and websites and moved money to the ads with the lowest cost per call.
  3. Separate funeral and cremation ads at each chapel
    We split each chapel's own search campaign into separate funeral and cremation ad groups, so funeral searchers see funeral ads and cremation searchers see cremation ads.
  4. Reached new families and languages
    Added cemetery searches, restored nearby areas, and added Chinese and Vietnamese video ads.

The website had 109 taps to call: 36 came from Google Ads and 37 from regular Google search.

Since Oct 26, Google Ads also counts website calls: someone clicks an ad, visits the site, then calls. It counted 12 of these calls through Nov 3: 11 to East Sacramento and 1 to Sierra Hills / Greenback.
