Questions families ask

Frequently asked questions

What should I do immediately when a loved one passes away?

Call East Lawn right away. We are available 24/7 to serve families across Sacramento, Roseville, and Elk Grove. We will transfer your loved one into our care, gather necessary information, and guide you through service planning. For Elk Grove, call (916) 732-2031.

Can we have a funeral service if we choose cremation?

Yes. Our Traditional Service includes visitation, a funeral service with your loved one present, and cremation afterward. Cremation may take place before or after the funeral.

Does East Lawn have its own crematory?

Yes. East Lawn operates its own crematory, ensuring that your loved one never leaves our care. Cremation is performed at our owned East Sacramento crematory.

Can family members witness the cremation?

Yes. Families are welcome to witness the cremation if they choose.

Can services be held on weekends or evenings?

Yes. We accommodate weekend and evening services for families throughout the greater Sacramento area.

Does East Lawn accept life insurance?

Yes, we work directly with life insurance companies on behalf of families in the Sacramento region.

What is the difference between a funeral, a memorial service, and a celebration of life?

A funeral usually has the body or ashes present, often followed by burial or cremation. A memorial service is similar but without the body. A celebration of life is typically less formal and focuses on sharing stories and memories.

We have a pre-need policy from another funeral home. Can East Lawn help?

Yes. We honor and accept preneed funeral policies from other funeral homes.
