Luminary Remembrance Program

You are invited to a peaceful evening of reflection at our Luminary Remembrance Program, held at all three East Lawn locations. Families will receive luminary bags and candles, provided by East Lawn, to decorate and place beside their loved one's memorial in a shared tribute of light and love.

Sunday, December 6, 2026

4:00 PM - 7:00 PM

The evening also includes a candle lighting ceremony and keepsake ornaments. Please join us at your family's East Lawn location.
