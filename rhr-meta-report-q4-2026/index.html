Where your ads show

  • Your ads center on Folsom, El Dorado Hills, and Fair Oaks. Meta requires a 15-mile minimum radius for remodeling ads, so nearby areas like Citrus Heights, Carmichael, and Rancho Cordova are included by default.

What's working

  • Your ads were seen 6,960 times by 3,883 people in the Sacramento area in 9 days.
  • 87 people visited your landing page from the ads, and 115 clicked through.
  • The Additions carousel and the Finished-Work Reel are the crowd favorites, bringing 65 of the 87 website visits.
  • People 55 and older are the most engaged group, with 67 of the 115 clicks.
  • Website visitors are being saved for retargeting, so we can follow up with people who already showed interest.

What we're watching

  • No call-button taps or form leads have been recorded on the landing page yet, so turning visits into calls is our top focus.
  • The $2,500 off video is the campaign's main ad, and starting Nov 4 it gets the full budget so more people see the offer.
  • Daily website visits have slowed after a strong first day (38 on Oct 26, 4 on Nov 3) as the same people see the ads again.

Next steps

  1. Now: Lead with the $2,500 off video
    The full budget goes to the offer video, focused on Folsom, El Dorado Hills, and Fair Oaks.
  2. Now: Save everyone who watches it
    People who watch most of the video are being saved into a follow-up audience.
  3. Wednesday, Nov 11: Follow up with your project photos
    Those viewers start seeing your finished kitchens, baths, additions, and exteriors, so the offer is backed by real work.
  4. Through Dec 31: Turn interest into calls
    We'll watch calls and estimate requests closely and keep adjusting until the offer ends.
