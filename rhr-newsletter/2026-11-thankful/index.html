Smoked Gouda Mac and Cheese

Recipe by Kathleen at Gonna Want Seconds

Why it brings everyone together

This is not the boxed stuff. Smoked Gouda and a little cream cheese make a silky, smoky cheese sauce, and it bakes under a buttery breadcrumb topping with crumbled bacon, Parmesan and fresh thyme. It serves 8 and takes about 70 minutes, start to finish.

Key ingredients

  • 1 pound dried elbow pasta
  • 4 cups shredded smoked Gouda
  • 4 ounces cream cheese
  • 1/2 cup shredded Parmesan
  • 5 1/2 cups whole milk
  • 1 stick unsalted butter and 1/2 cup flour
  • Dijon mustard, nutmeg and a pinch of cayenne
  • 2 cups panko bread crumbs
  • 8 slices cooked, crumbled bacon
  • Fresh thyme leaves
