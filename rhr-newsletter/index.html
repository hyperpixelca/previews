TRICK-OR-TREAT SAFETY

Get your home ready for trick-or-treaters

1
Light the way. Check your porch and yard lights, replace burned-out bulbs, and keep the path to your door well lit.
2
Clear the path. Move garden hoses, toys and bikes off the porch and walkway so no one trips in the dark.
3
Sweep the steps. Wet leaves on sidewalks and steps are slippery. A quick sweep helps prevent slips and falls.
4
Go flameless. Light jack-o-lanterns with battery candles, flashlights or glow sticks, and keep any candlelit pumpkins off the porch and walkways.
5
Be bright and be seen. Choose bright, reflective costumes, add reflective tape to costumes and treat bags, and carry a flashlight after dark.
6
Walk smart. Stay on sidewalks and well-lit streets, cross as a group at crosswalks, and go with young children on their rounds.
7
Check treats at home. Sort candy before anyone digs in, and toss anything unwrapped, spoiled or suspicious.

Tips adapted from the American Academy of Pediatrics at HealthyChildren.org.

HALLOWEEN TREAT

Monster Eyeball Pretzel Bites

Recipe inspired by My Heavenly Recipes

Ingredients (makes 36)

  • 36 dark chocolate Rolos (or regular Rolos)
  • 36 pretzel snaps or mini twists
  • 24 M&Ms
  • 12 large candy eyes

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 250 degrees F and line two baking trays with parchment paper.
  2. Place the pretzels on the trays and top each one with an unwrapped Rolo.
  3. Bake one tray for about 2 to 3 minutes, just enough to soften the Rolos without fully melting them.
  4. Remove from the oven and immediately press a candy eye or M&M on top of each Rolo. Repeat with the second tray.
  5. Let them set 20 to 30 minutes at room temperature, or chill 10 to 15 minutes to speed things up.
