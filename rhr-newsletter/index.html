HALLOWEEN TREAT

Jack-o'-Lantern Pumpkin Spice Monster Cookies

Ingredients (makes about 24)

  • 1 cup butter, softened
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup pumpkin puree
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla
  • 2 1/2 cups all purpose flour
  • 2 tsp pumpkin pie spice
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 cup orange and purple candy coated chocolates
  • Candy eyeballs

Steps

  1. Heat the oven to 350F and line two baking sheets.
  2. Cream the butter and brown sugar, then beat in the pumpkin, egg and vanilla.
  3. Mix in the flour, spice, baking soda and salt, then fold in the candies.
  4. Scoop 2 tablespoon balls onto the sheets and bake 11 to 13 minutes.
  5. Press candy eyeballs on while the cookies are warm. Cool and serve.
