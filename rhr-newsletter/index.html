Swirled Pumpkin Spice Bark

Recipe inspired by Dinner, then Dessert

Ingredients (makes about 12 pieces)

  • 12 oz white chocolate melting wafers
  • 6 soft caramels, unwrapped
  • 3 tbsp pumpkin puree
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla
  • 1/2 tsp pumpkin pie spice
  • 2 tbsp graham cracker crumbs
  • 2 tbsp coarse sanding sugar

Steps

  1. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Melt the caramels, then stir in the pumpkin, vanilla and pumpkin pie spice until smooth.
  3. Melt the white chocolate and spread it on the parchment in a thin rectangle, about 10 by 14 inches.
  4. Spoon the pumpkin caramel over the top, sprinkle on the graham crumbs and swirl gently with a knife.
  5. Dust with sanding sugar, chill about 10 minutes until firm, then break into pieces.
