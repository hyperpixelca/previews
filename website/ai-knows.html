    HOW WE CHECK THE LEADERBOARD

    Same questions. Same tools. Same rules for everyone.

    01

    The 10 most-searched categories

    Each month's categories are the types of local business people in the Sacramento area search for most on Google. We recheck every month, and a category that moves up gets a New this month tag.

    02

    3 local questions, 3 AI tools, 10 times

    Each category gets three everyday questions a local customer would ask. We ask ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini each one 10 times, because AI answers change from run to run.

    03

    Sacramento area only

    Only businesses headquartered in the Sacramento area count. National chains are left out, even when AI names them.

    04

    Top 5, refreshed monthly

    We show the top 5 per category with an as-of date. Hyper Pixel clients are marked as clients.
