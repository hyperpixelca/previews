FAQ

Questions

Straight answers about how an AI team works in your business.

Is this a chatbot?

No. A chatbot answers questions on a website. This is a team of agents that each run a whole job inside your business, like intake, follow-up or reporting, set up and managed by us.

Will an agent message my customers on its own?

Not unless you choose that. Until you say otherwise, anything going to a customer waits for your approval.

Do I have to switch tools?

The audit starts with the tools and accounts you already use. Your written agent plan lists which tools each agent connects to.

Where does my data live?

In your own accounts. Every action is logged, and there are spending caps and a pause switch.

What happens after launch?

We manage your agents every month. We monitor them, tune them as your business changes and send you a written review.
