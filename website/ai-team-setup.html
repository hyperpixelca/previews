FAQ

Questions

Straight answers about how an AI team works in your business.

Can't I just set this up myself?

You can, if you have the time to research the tools, learn them and keep them running. We build it, connect it to your tools and manage it every month, so it stays off your plate.

What does it cost each month?

Monthly management starts at $1,500 for a two-agent team, $3,000 for four to five agents and from $5,000 for enterprise teams. Setup is 50% off with a 12-month agreement. AI and software subscriptions are billed to your own accounts.

Is my data safe?

Your data stays in your own accounts. Every action is logged, and there are spending caps and a pause switch.

How long does setup take?

It starts with a workflow audit. Then we build your agents, test them on real examples and launch with team training. Timing depends on your team size and is confirmed after the audit.
