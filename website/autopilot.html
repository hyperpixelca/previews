How it works

Every item is checked by a person against your fact sheet before it goes out.

01

Pick a plan and pay online

Checkout is handled by Stripe. No sales call needed.

02

Fill in the intake form

Your business details, brand, photos, and an invite for our account on your website, Google profile and email platform.

03

Setup week

A starting AI search check, your email template, schema and FAQ fixes, and a one-page fact sheet on your business.

04

We draft, a person reviews

Our team drafts each item. A Hyper Pixel reviewer approves it. Nothing posts on its own.

05

Publish and report

Approved items go out on schedule. A one-page report arrives on the 1st of each month.
