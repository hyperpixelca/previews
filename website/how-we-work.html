How we work

Say what we will do. Then do it

Four steps. One plan.

hyperpixel. plans, makes and runs marketing for businesses that want more customers. Here is exactly how that works, from the first call to your live dashboard.

The process

Plan / Make / Run / Report
01

PlanWe learn the business and set the plan.

  • Book a call. We learn your goals, your customers and your busy seasons.
  • Pick your credits: 3 services, 5 services or all 7.
  • Fill out one onboarding form. Only the steps you need show up.
  • Connect your accounts by invite. No passwords shared.
02

MakeWe create the content.

  • A three-hour photo and video shoot every month, in every plan.
  • Posts, reels, pages, emails and ads built from your brand.
  • Real photos of your business, not stock.
  • A person checks every piece before it goes out.
03

RunWe publish and manage it.

  • Posts go out on schedule, up to 6 edited reels a month.
  • Comment replies on Facebook and Instagram.
  • Reviews answered and ad budgets watched every week.
  • Site updates, security and backups handled for you.
04

ReportWe show you the numbers.

  • A live client dashboard you can check any time.
  • One panel for each service in your plan.
  • What worked, what didn't and what we do next.
