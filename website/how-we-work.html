How we work

Say what we will do. Then do it

Four steps. One plan.

We plan, make and run the marketing services you choose. Start with your goals, agree the scope and keep people responsible for the decisions.

The process

Plan / Make / Run / Report
01

PlanWe learn the business and agree the scope.

  • Book a call to talk through your goals, customers and busy seasons.
  • Choose one service or build a mix. There is no minimum package, and Content is optional.
  • Confirm deliverables, responsibilities, fees and the approval process in your quote.
  • Share brand assets and arrange the account access needed for your selected services.
02

MakeWe make the work your plan needs.

  • If you choose Content, the proposed scope includes one three-hour photo and video shoot each month.
  • For Social without Content, you supply usable photos and video; Social includes up to six edited reels a month.
  • We create the posts, pages, emails or ads covered by your selected services.
  • People review drafts and edits before the work moves to publishing.
03

RunWe manage the services you selected.

  • Follow the agreed publishing and client-approval workflow for your selected channels.
  • Handle site care, campaigns or review responses where those services are in scope.
  • Ad spend is separate from management fees; budgets and changes stay under human oversight.
  • Keep requests outside the agreed scope separate, so you can approve any extra work.
04

ReportWe explain what happened and what comes next.

  • Report on the services in your plan, with a reporting format and cadence agreed during onboarding.
  • Check the source data and show the dates behind the numbers.
  • Explain what worked, what needs attention and what we recommend next.
  • People review the findings and discuss the next decisions with you.

FAQ

Questions

Straight answers about choosing services, supplying content and getting the work approved.

Do I need to choose three services?

No. Choose one service or any mix of the seven services. The three- and five-service suggestions are starting points, not minimums. Content is optional.

What's included if I choose Content?

The proposed Content scope is one three-hour photo and video shoot a month, with lightly edited photos and captured video clips. Social editing and publishing are a separate service. The final shoot scope is confirmed in your quote.

Can I choose Social without a monthly shoot?

Yes. You supply usable photos and video if you choose Social without Content. The proposed Social scope includes up to six edited reels a month for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. We confirm the asset requirements before work begins.

How long until my first content goes live?

We agree a start date after the scope, account access, usable assets and approval steps are confirmed. Timing depends on the services you choose and any production needed; we do not promise a fixed turnaround before that review.

Do I need a long-term contract?

The current proposed offer gives our best offer on a one-year agreement, with a six-month option also available. Fees, discounts and terms are confirmed in your quote before you commit.

Do I approve everything before it posts?

We agree your approval process during onboarding, including what needs your sign-off and how revisions are handled. People review creative before publishing. AI support does not replace human accountability or your agreed approvals.
