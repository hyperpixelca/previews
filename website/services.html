FAQ

Content and Social questions

What's included if I choose Content?

Content includes one three-hour photo and video shoot a month, with lightly edited photos and captured video clips. Our team shoots everything in person on Sony cameras and DJI gear. Social editing and publishing are a separate service. The final shoot scope is confirmed in your quote.

Can I choose Social without a monthly shoot?

Yes. You supply usable photos and video if you choose Social without Content. Social includes 12 to 15 posts a month, a mix of photos, carousels and up to six edited reels, for Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. We confirm the asset requirements before work begins.
